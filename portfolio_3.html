Geo Trip Planner

Problem
Pilgrims struggle to plan multi-stop Tirupati trips, pick the right budget tier, and track temple visits without a simple digital flow.
Solution
Budget-based packages, a place picker, an interactive route map, personal temple QR check-in, and an admin visit dashboard.
Outcome
Users plan trips faster and share routes on the map; staff scan QR codes to log visits, and admins see visitor data in one place.

PIN · 02

Geo Intel Meta Hub

Problem
Scattered geospatial data — vectors, rasters, LAS, 3D — is hard to store, publish and view in one place.
Solution
A Flask app that uploads files into PostGIS + GeoServer, surfaced through a web UI for search, maps and 3D viewing.
Outcome
A central catalog with WMS/WFS support (plus rasters and 3D) so users can discover, preview and serve layers from one platform.
